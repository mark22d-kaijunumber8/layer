Support

Something not working, or an idea for Layer? Email notch.app.support@gmail.com and you'll get a reply.

How do I add a product?

On the Shelf tab, tap the scan button. Choose Label to photograph the ingredient list on the back, Barcode to look the product up, or Shelfie to photograph several products at once. You can also tap + to search by name or paste an ingredient list. Your routine updates as soon as the product is on your shelf.

The box doesn't list all the ingredients.

Take a screenshot of the product's page on the brand's or a shop's website, where the full list and the directions usually are. Then open the product on your shelf and tap Update from a screenshot. Layer reads the ingredients and the directions from it.

What is skin cycling?

Strong actives work best when they take turns. Layer rotates exfoliation nights, retinoid nights and recovery nights, so an acid and a retinoid never land on the same night and your skin gets nights off to recover. For sensitive skin, it adds an extra recovery night.

How do I see tomorrow's routine?

On Today, tap the calendar button at the top right. Each night ahead shows its type, and tapping one shows the steps for that night. The sun and moon switch at the top flips between the morning and evening routine.

Why is a product "resting" tonight?

It's on your shelf but doesn't suit tonight. On a retinoid night, for example, an acid rests because using both together raises the risk of irritation. The reason is shown under each product, and it comes back on a night it suits.

A product has run out.

Open it on your shelf and tap Mark as finished. Layer takes it out of your routine and moves it to the rebuy list on your shelf. When you have a new one, tap Restock.

How do I share my routine with a friend?

On the Shelf, tap the share button at the top left, add your name if you like, and tap Share. Your friend gets a picture of your routine with your product photos, and a link that shows each step, morning and night, and how you use each product. If they have Layer, the link opens straight in the app, where they can add any product they also own. Otherwise it opens a web page with the same routine. Nothing is uploaded: the routine travels inside the link, and the picture goes only where you send it.

The weather card isn't showing.

Tap Set location on the weather card. Choose Use my location, or search for your city if you'd rather not share your location. Layer uses it only for the day's UV and humidity.

Will I lose my shelf if I get a new phone?

Layer keeps everything on your iPhone, and it's included in your iPhone's backups. Restore your new phone from a backup and your shelf, routine and journal come with it. Deleting the app deletes them.

Is this medical advice?

No. Layer gives general skincare information based on published research. For a skin condition, a reaction or a prescription treatment, follow your doctor or dermatologist.
