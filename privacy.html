Layer Support Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026

Layer is a skincare routine app for iPhone, made by Lun Do. The short version: there's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your shelf, routine, journal photos and Coach conversations stay on your iPhone. Layer only goes online to look up the weather and products, and those requests carry no information about who you are.

What stays on your iPhone

Your products, their photos and ingredient lists, your skin type and goals, your routine and the steps you tick off, your Coach conversations and your journal photos are stored only on your iPhone. Ingredient labels and screenshots you scan are read on the phone itself. Nobody else can see any of it, including us.

Like the rest of your apps, Layer's data is part of your iPhone's backups, to iCloud or to a computer, if you have backups turned on. Apple handles those backups under its own privacy policy. Deleting Layer, or choosing Reset in its settings, deletes everything it stored.

What Layer sends, and to whom

  • Weather, to Open-Meteo: to check the day's UV and humidity, Layer sends your approximate location, rounded to about 10 km, to Open-Meteo. If you search for a city instead, it sends the name you type. Nothing else goes with it. This is optional: you can skip weather and Layer works without it.
  • Product lookups, to Open Beauty Facts: when you scan a barcode or search for a product by name, Layer sends the barcode number or the words you typed to Open Beauty Facts, a free, open database of cosmetics, to get the product's name and ingredients.

Neither request includes your name, an account, a device identifier, or anything from your shelf or journal. Both services have their own privacy policies.

Sharing your routine

If you tap Share your routine on the Shelf, Layer puts the first name you choose and your routine (each product's name, brand, type, key ingredients and when you use it, in order) into the link itself, after the # sign. Nothing is uploaded: browsers don't send that part of a link to any website, so the page that shows it reads it on the recipient's device, and neither we nor GitHub, which hosts the page, receive it. Your skin goals and settings are never included. Along with the link, Layer makes a picture of your routine on your phone, with your product photos, and hands both to the share sheet. Layer doesn't upload the picture either: it goes only to the people and apps you send it to. Anyone who has the link or picture can see the routine, so send them only to people you choose. Nothing is shared unless you tap Share.

Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan ingredient labels, barcodes and photos of your shelf, and for the weekly progress photo in Journal. Photos are saved on your phone.
  • Location: used, only while you're using the app, to look up the day's UV and humidity as described above. Layer doesn't store your location history.
  • Photos: you pick photos with Apple's photo picker, and Layer only gets the ones you choose. It can't see the rest of your library.
  • Notifications: used for the reminders you turn on, like reapplying sunscreen or the weekly progress photo. They're scheduled on your phone.

Children

Layer collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If a future version of Layer works differently, for example by syncing your shelf between devices, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
