Layer

The skincare you already own, in the right order.

Add your products and Layer reads their ingredients, works out which ones pair well and which shouldn't share a night, and builds your morning and evening routine step by step. Exfoliation, retinoid and recovery nights take turns on their own, so strong actives never pile up.

What's inside

  • Scan an ingredient label, a barcode or your whole shelf, or import a screenshot of a product page.
  • Skin cycling planned for you, with a calendar of the nights ahead.
  • Routines that change with your skin type and up to four goals.
  • The research behind every rule, with a link to each study.
  • The day's UV and humidity, factored into your routine.
  • What your shelf covers, and what's worth adding next.
  • A Coach that answers from your own shelf and plan.
  • A weekly progress journal with side-by-side photos.

Layer is free for iPhone. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and your shelf, routine and photos stay on your phone.

Layer gives general skincare information based on published research. It isn't medical advice.
